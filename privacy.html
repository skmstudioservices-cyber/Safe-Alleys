Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · 26 September 2026 · Plain-language summary first, details below.

In one sentence

Your personal profile never leaves your device; we only ever receive anonymous community safety reports, and we never sell, rent, or personalise anything.

What stays on your device (never uploaded)

  • Your age band, gender, travel modes, and saved routes — stored in your browser's IndexedDB on your device only.
  • Your location while you use the map. Location is read by your browser, shown to you, and never transmitted to us during normal map browsing.
  • Your language and display preferences (browser localStorage).

What we receive (anonymous reports only)

  • When you submit a report: the DIGIPIN + coordinates of the spot, the category you picked, time-of-day bucket, and an optional 200-character note (filtered).
  • A rotating anonymous device token used only to rate-limit votes per device. It is not linked to your name, phone, email, or location history.
  • Age band and gender are attached only in aggregated form for statistics — they are never displayed individually and never used for advertising.

What we never do

  • No accounts, no login, no phone number, no email required.
  • No tracking of your location history, ever.
  • No advertising personalisation — ads, if ever shown on non-critical screens, are contextual only and never use your profile.
  • No selling or sharing of individual-level data — because individual-level data does not exist on our servers.

Your rights (India, DPDP Act 2023)

You can access, correct, and delete everything at any time:

  • Device data: clear your browser storage for this site, or use the in-app export/import (encrypted) to move or back it up yourself.
  • Reports: reports are anonymous by design. Once submitted, they are aggregated; contact us via GitHub if you need a specific report removed.
  • Consent: the first sync asks your permission in plain language (English + Hindi), and you choose the sync schedule — or never sync.

Third parties

  • OpenStreetMap Foundation serves the map tiles (standard OSM privacy policy applies; your IP is visible to them as with any website).
  • Cloudflare hosts the site (standard CDN logging applies).
  • No analytics, no ad SDKs, no social pixels in Phase 0–2.

Safety of children

Safe-Alleys is designed so children can use a parent's phone without accounts or payments. We knowingly collect no personal data from children — or from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the app will show the new version before your next sync. This page always carries the current version.

Disclaimer

Safe-Alleys is an advisory community tool. We never mark any place "safe" or "unsafe" as a fact — we show where people have reported feeling unsafe. No data means not enough data. This is not an emergency service; in an emergency call 112 (India).
