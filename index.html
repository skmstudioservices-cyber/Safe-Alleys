How it will work

👁️

1. See

Open the map. Colours show where people report feeling unsafe — like a traffic layer.

📍

2. Report

Tap the spot, pick an icon — unlit stretch, harassment, theft, animals, flooding — done. No login.

👍

3. Verify

People nearby confirm “still like this today?” with one tap. Community verification keeps data fresh.

Privacy-first, by design

  • 🔒 Your profile (age band, gender, routes) lives only in your device's IndexedDB — it never leaves your phone.
  • 🕵️ The server receives only anonymised reports. No accounts, no tracking, no ad personalisation.
  • 💸 Free forever. Children on parents' phones can use it — no payment methods, no friction.
