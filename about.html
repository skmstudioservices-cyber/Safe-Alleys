About Safe-Alleys

A free, no-login, privacy-first map showing where people report feeling unsafe — at alley-level precision using DIGIPIN, India Post's official digital address system. Colour-coded like a traffic map, so you can understand it without reading.

🔓 No login💸 Free forever🛡️ Private by design🇮🇳 India-first
🗺️ Open the map

How it works

👁️

1. See

Colours show where people report feeling unsafe — like a traffic layer.

📍

2. Report

Tap the spot, pick an icon — unlit stretch, harassment, theft, animals, flooding. No login.

👍

3. Verify

People nearby confirm with one tap. Community verification keeps data fresh.

Every spot is identified by its DIGIPIN — a 10-character digital address accurate to about 4 metres. Tap anywhere on the map to see the DIGIPIN of that spot.

What the colours mean

  • 🟡 Some reports
  • 🟠 Medium reports
  • 🔴 High reports
  • ⚫ Not enough data

There is no green. We never label any place "safe" — no data means not enough data.

Your privacy

  • 🔒 Your profile (age band, gender, routes) lives only in your device — it never leaves your phone.
  • 🕵️ The server receives only anonymised reports. No accounts, no tracking, no ad personalisation.
  • 📄 Read the full privacy policy →

Important — what this is NOT

  • ❌ Not an emergency service — in an emergency call 112 (India).
  • ❌ Not a "safe place" certification — we never mark places safe.
  • ❌ Not a tracking or surveillance app — there is no location tracking, ever.
